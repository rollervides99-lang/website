Executive overview

Quantitative machine learning for fundamental equity research.

Dakota is a quantitative machine learning model developed to support the fundamental equity research process.

While investment ideas are generated through traditional research and valuation, Dakota uses historical financial and market data to estimate expected next-quarter returns, quantify forecast uncertainty, and derive implied price targets.

These forecasts serve as an additional quantitative input, helping analysts and portfolio managers evaluate investment opportunities with greater confidence while complementing—not replacing—fundamental analysis.
