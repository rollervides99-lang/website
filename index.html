Dakota project

Dakota is a quantitative research framework designed to estimate next-quarter equity returns using a combination of financial, technical, macroeconomic, and market-based data. The model combines multiple machine-learning approaches through a meta-learner to produce expected returns, directional probabilities, and risk-aware signals that can support investment research and portfolio decision-making.
